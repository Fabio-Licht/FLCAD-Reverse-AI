# FLCAD Reverse AI

## Project Charter

**Project Name**

FLCAD Reverse AI

**Tagline**

The Intelligent Engineering Platform

---

# Mission

Develop the world's most intelligent engineering platform, integrating Reverse Engineering, CAD, Artificial Intelligence, Mesh Processing, Inspection and Digital Manufacturing into a single ecosystem.

---

# Vision

Become one of the world's leading engineering platforms, enabling engineers to transform scanned reality into intelligent manufacturing solutions with the assistance of Artificial Intelligence.

---

# Purpose

Engineering software has remained fragmented for decades.

FLCAD Reverse AI exists to unify engineering workflows into a single intelligent platform where geometry, engineering knowledge and Artificial Intelligence work together.
